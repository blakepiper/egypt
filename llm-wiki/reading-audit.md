# Second-pass reading audit

This page records what was actually read for the rebuilt wiki. It exists because “a file was indexed” is not the same as “its content was incorporated.” The archive contains 72 substantive files grouped into 36 intellectual sources in [[source-catalog]]. Duplicate exports and successive drafts were compared rather than counted as independent authorities.

## Reading standard

**Full reading** means all recoverable prose was read from beginning to end. For scanned PDFs, this combined page-aware OCR with inspection of the page images; OCR was used to find and understand content, not trusted for exact quotation. For slides and visual packets, all slides/pages were inspected and their extractable text was read.

**Full comparative reading** means every version was extracted and compared, with the longest or latest version read continuously and every shorter version checked for unique wording, additions, omissions, or contamination. This is the appropriate standard for drafts that repeat most of the same paper.

**Structured sampling** is reserved for long books and nonassigned research references. It means reading the complete contents, introduction or framing, relevant chapter openings and conclusions, and targeted passages tied to the syllabus, exams, or student projects. The user explicitly allowed this treatment for long textbooks.

## Visual and duplicate-file controls

- The Amduat and Book of Gates were rendered as complete twelve-page contact sheets so that low-text pages could not disappear from the audit.
- The Plate 30 and presentation files were checked as visual documents, not judged only by text extraction.
- The Exam 1 files are not identical saves. The fuller version contains more answers and corrects Wadjet from an earlier wrong match to the cobra identification.
- The Plate 30 ODT is contaminated after its assignment text by an early water-lily draft. The wiki records that as file history and routes the prose to the correct project.
- The water-lily finals are overlapping but not identical. The longest final is the interpretive base, while unique early phrasing and the proposal/bibliography stages are preserved in [[student-work-reconstruction]].
- The Set drafts preserve genuine argument development; they were not collapsed into a single undifferentiated “report.”

## What “incorporated” means

Incorporation does not mean copying every sentence. It means every substantive topic, distinctive claim, correction, uncertainty, and assignment expectation has a findable home. Boilerplate, repeated citations, formatting artifacts, and duplicate wording are cataloged but not reproduced. Student claims remain visible even when corrected; they are never silently converted into scholarly consensus.
