# Questions and evidence

Recurring questions in the archive concern the relationship between ritual, images, bodies, names, and places.

## Creation and renewal

[[creation-traditions]] and [[solar-cycle]] describe different accounts of beginnings and recurring renewal. Their differences reflect local traditions and changing evidence.

## Kingship and ritual

[[maat-isfet-and-kingship]] and [[temples-priests-and-offerings]] connect political responsibility with offerings and divine service.

## Death and personhood

[[personhood-and-the-afterlife]] and [[funerary-text-tradition]] explain the distinct needs of bodies, names, and other aspects of a person. No single text represents every Egyptian burial.
