# Book of the Dead Plate 30

The student's report identifies Plate 30 as the opening of Chapter 125: entry into the Hall of the Two Truths and praise of Osiris. Its immediate issue is **vindication through authenticated knowledge**.

## Scene

The surviving image shows Osiris mummiform beneath a canopy, with Isis behind him and the Four Sons of Horus before him. In the full report's comparison images, Ani and Tutu approach and make offerings in related plates. Osiris holds royal/regenerative insignia; Isis supplies protective presence; the Sons of Horus connect the judgment setting to preserved bodily organs.

The standalone `Plate 30.jpg` is a cropped right-hand portion, while `Unified.jpg` joins adjacent source images for the student's analysis. The `Plate 30.pdf` and presentation contain slide-like image material rather than a full searchable translation.

## Textual movement

1. Ani is titled “the Osiris, scribe Ani,” receiving the status of the transfigured dead.
2. He praises Osiris and claims primordial/secret knowledge.
3. Anubis testifies that Ani knows roads and towns and is acceptable to the gods.
4. Ani invokes a dense ritual geography: Mendes/Banebdjedet, Satis's house, Neshmet bark, Athribis, Busiris, Anubis's embalming domain, Rosetau, and Naref.
5. The gate and its upper/lower portions demand their names.
6. Correct naming produces passage and the status “vindicated, possessor of reverence.”

The report gives the gate name “You Dispel Light,” with upper and lower names “Lord of Truth, Master of His Two Legs” and “Lord of Strength, the One Who Commands the Cattle.” Translation and segmentation can vary by edition.

## What the scene teaches

**Knowledge is identity.** Ani does not solve a riddle for its own sake. By knowing divine topography and names, he demonstrates that he belongs within the ordered otherworld.

**Architecture is animate.** Gate, doorposts, guardians, and regions possess names. The built threshold is a being and relationship, not inert scenery.

**Vindication is sponsored and enacted.** Anubis's testimony, Ani's speech, Osirian identification, offerings, image, and secret names cooperate.

**Corpora overlap.** Osirian identification recalls Pyramid and Coffin Texts; gate passwords recall the Book of Gates; judgment develops across Chapter 125 and related Book of the Dead material. This is the “shingles” model in one plate.

## Correction to the student report

The report calls the vignette-like image a “short mythological retelling”; in standard usage a vignette is the illustration attached to a spell, while a short empowering myth is a **historiola**. The course exam appears to blur those two terms in one answer. The wiki keeps them distinct.
