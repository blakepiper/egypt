# Religion of Ancient Egypt

This archive brings together a sourced guide to ancient Egyptian religion. It draws on ancient texts in translation, modern scholarship, visual material, and a reviewed source collection. The material is consolidated in `../raw/`, with source notes marking where each claim comes from and where uncertainty remains.
